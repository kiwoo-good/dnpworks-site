Home Tidy

Keeps the home screen you already use and sorts its scattered icons into folders and most-used order.

How it works

1
ReadIt reads where your icons and folders are now.
2
PreviewIt shows you the plan before anything moves.
3
TidyOnce you approve, it moves the icons and builds the folders.
4
UndoNot what you wanted? It puts everything back.

Usage access (optional)
