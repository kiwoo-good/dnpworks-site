SPLIT

Split

A running app that builds a training plan for your goal race and adjusts it to what you actually run.

OverviewScreensRequirementsNoticesPrivacy policyContact

What it does

01

A plan built for your goal

Enter your race date and target time, and it lays out the training week by week until race day.

02

Adjusts to what you ran

It reads your watch records and reworks the rest of the plan when you ran less or more than planned.

03

A room that fills as you run

Your runs and race medals collect in your room.

Screens

Screens are shown in Korean. The records are examples.
