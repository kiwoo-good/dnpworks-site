What is Home Tidy?

An Android app that tidies the icons on the home screen you already use, with no launcher to switch to. It shows you the plan first and moves nothing until you approve.

  • Works with Samsung One UI Home and Pixel Launcher
  • Undo everything in one tap
  • Stops at once when a call comes in
