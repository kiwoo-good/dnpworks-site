Split Privacy Policy

Split reads your running records, builds a training plan toward your goal race, and adjusts it to what you actually ran. The current version has no accounts. Your health data and running records are processed only on your phone, and the training plan is computed on your phone.

We do not collect: your name, phone number, email or any account identity, contacts, running routes (GPS tracks), step counts, sleep records, or advertising identifiers.

2. Transfers, third parties and processors

In the current version, health data, running records, photos and the sentences you type do not leave your phone, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell this information to third parties.

The current version of the app connects to the internet only as listed below. As with any connection, the receiving server sees your IP address at that moment.

4. Limits on the use of health data

  • Data read from Apple Health and Health Connect is used only to build and adjust your training plan.
  • It is not used for advertising, marketing, data sales or credit decisions.
  • It is not used to train artificial intelligence models.
  • Predicted times and training plans shown by Split are calculated reference values, not medical diagnosis or prescription.

5. Garmin Connect integration (in preparation)

This feature is not available yet. When it launches we will finalize this section, announce it, and ask for separate consent in the app. The plan is as follows.

  • It is optional. It works only after you tap "Connect Garmin" in the app and approve access in your Garmin account.
  • Data received: your Garmin user identifier, access tokens, and running activity records (type, start time, distance, duration, pace, heart rate, laps).
  • Data sent: training sessions created by Split (for example, threshold 3×2 km) are sent to Garmin Connect so you can run them as workouts on your Garmin device.
  • Garmin delivers records to Split's server. The server keeps a record only until your phone has fetched it, and deletes it after delivery.
  • Data received from Garmin is used only to build and adjust that user's training plan. It is not shared with third parties or used to train artificial intelligence.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app or in Garmin Connect. When you disconnect, the tokens and any records left on the server are deleted.

6. Your rights

  • All information is on your phone, so you can view, edit and delete it in the app.
  • You can turn off health data access at any time in your phone's settings (Health app or Health Connect).
  • Deleting the app deletes everything the app stored.
  • A file created with "Export backup" in settings is kept by you. Split does not receive it.
  • For privacy questions, write to the email address below.

7. Children

Split is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current version. If a new feature such as accounts, payments, advertising, or a connection to an external device or service adds to the information processed or sent outside the phone, we will update this policy on this page with a new effective date and give notice in the app before that feature launches. Where consent is required, we will ask for it separately in the app. The limits on health data in section 4 will continue to apply as features are added.
