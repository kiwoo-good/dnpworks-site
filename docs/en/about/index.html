About

We'll do the tidying

D&P Co.,Ltd builds apps that take over the tidying you keep putting off, on your phone and in your day.

What we do

Scattered icons, piled-up photos, a training plan left behind: chores everyone means to do and never gets around to. D&P builds apps that do them for you.

Our first app is Home Tidy, which organizes your home screen. Next are Split, a running coach, and Photo Diet, a photo tidying app (both working titles).

Our principles

01

It stays on your device

We handle only what a feature needs and keep it on your phone wherever we can.

02

Show first, then act

You see exactly what will change, and only what you approve gets done.

03

Always undoable

We remember how things were and put them back whenever you ask.
