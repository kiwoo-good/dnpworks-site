Home Tidy Privacy Policy

Home Tidy rearranges the icons and folders on your phone's home screen according to a plan you approve. The current version has no internet permission; everything below is processed only on your phone.

We do not collect: identity information such as name, phone number or account, location, contacts, photos or files, the content of other apps' screens, typed text, passwords, or notification content.

2. Transfer, sharing and outsourcing

None. The current version has no internet permission and cannot send this information off the device. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The app's data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

3. Use of the AccessibilityService API

  • What it reads: names and positions of icons, folders and widgets shown on the home screen and Apps screen of the default home app (Samsung One UI Home, Pixel Launcher).
  • What it does: only when you tap Tidy, Undo or Add, it performs screen gestures (tap, long press, drag, pinch) to move icons, create, name or take apart folders, delete or add empty pages, and change the sort mode of the Apps screen.
  • While running it shows progress and a Stop button over the screen and blocks other touches.
  • Only while a tidy or undo is running, it checks which app owns the window on screen and whether a new notification is a call notification, so it can stop at once when a call comes in or the phone is folded or unfolded. Notification content is not read.
  • It never uninstalls or disables apps.
  • Information read through the accessibility service is used only to tidy the home screen, never for advertising or analytics.
  • Until you agree to the in-app disclosure, the service does nothing even if it is turned on. You can withdraw consent at any time from "See the accessibility notice again" at the bottom of the app's first screen.

5. Your rights and deletion

You can turn off each permission in the phone's settings at any time. Settings > Apps > Home Tidy > Storage > Clear data, or uninstalling the app, removes all stored information. The operator holds none of your information.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect children's personal information.

7. Changes

Changes are posted on this page with an effective date. If a change adds information we process, the app asks for your consent again. The same applies before any version that adds internet access or advertising: this policy is updated first and the app asks for your consent again.
